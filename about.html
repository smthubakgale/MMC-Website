Our Story

Founded in 2005 by Dr. Sarah Johnson and a team of dedicated healthcare professionals, MediCare Pulse began as a small clinic with a big vision - to revolutionize patient care through personalized treatment plans and cutting-edge medical technology.

Today, we've grown into a leading healthcare provider with multiple specialties under one roof, serving over 50,000 patients annually.

"Our mission is to provide accessible, high-quality healthcare that puts patients first."
