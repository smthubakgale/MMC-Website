Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services

Our regular operating hours are Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and Saturdays from 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays. For after-hours emergencies, please call our emergency line at (011) 234-5678.

While walk-ins are welcome, we highly recommend making an appointment to reduce waiting times. You can book appointments online through our website, via WhatsApp, or by calling our reception during business hours.

We accept cash, all major credit/debit cards, EFT payments, and medical aid claims. For your convenience, we can also process payments through SnapScan and Zapper. A booking fee is required for all appointments.

For chronic medication, we require an in-person or telemedicine consultation at least once every 6 months. You can request prescription renewals through our patient portal, by phone, or via WhatsApp. Please allow 48 hours for processing.

We provide urgent care services during operating hours for non-life-threatening conditions. For true medical emergencies (chest pain, severe bleeding, difficulty breathing, etc.), please go directly to the nearest hospital emergency room or call emergency services.

Please bring your ID/passport, medical aid card (if applicable), a list of current medications, and any relevant medical records. For new patients, we recommend arriving 15 minutes early to complete registration forms.
